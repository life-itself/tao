# Life Itself Tao

The handbook for [Life Itself](https://lifeitself.org/) — our tao (way), culture, strategy and general ways of working.

Current initiatives and planning conventions live in [Life Itself Planning](https://github.com/life-itself/planning). Its guide [How we plan and track work](https://planning.lifeitself.org/guides/how-we-plan) explains where things live, how to start a project and where tasks go. No Tao issue is required to start a project.

Published at https://tao.lifeitself.org

## What's here

- [`plans/`](plans/) — weekly operational plans
- [`meetings/`](meetings/) — meeting notes
- [`scqh.md`](scqh.md) and [`narrative.md`](narrative.md) — our public strategy summary

- [`strategy/`](strategy/) — annual plans (2018–2026) and the governance doc

Strategy work in progress and the strategy archive live in the private [life-itself/strategy](https://github.com/life-itself/strategy) repo. Finalised public strategy is added back here.

Portfolio, initiatives and people profiles live in [Planning](https://github.com/life-itself/planning).

The rest of the repo is the Tao itself: culture, principles, practices, ops, onboarding.
